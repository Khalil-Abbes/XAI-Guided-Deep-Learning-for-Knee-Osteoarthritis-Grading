## Group Members
* Mohamed Khalil Abbes
* Amirmahdi Ansaripour

## Dataset(s) Used
* **Primary Dataset:** `SilpaCS/kneeosteoarthritis` (Sourced via Hugging Face)
* **Description:** 8,260 pre-processed and cropped knee X-ray images (224x224 px) labeled with Kellgren-Lawrence grades (0-4).

## Research Question
Can explainable AI (XAI) methods, such as saliency maps, actively improve a convolutional neural network's diagnostic faithfulness and accuracy by identifying and mitigating incorrect visual cues during the training process?
